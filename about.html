// the story

I started in design,
now I build what I imagine.

I'm BLUEY — a UI/UX Designer with 3 years of experience crafting digital products in Figma. From wireframes to polished prototypes, I've spent years obsessing over layout, typography, user flows, and micro-interactions.

About a year ago, I decided to close the gap between design and engineering. I picked up HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, then moved into React. Now I build the things I used to just design — and the combination makes me a different kind of collaborator.

Backend is next. I'm actively learning Node.js and databases so I can go fullstack and own a product end to end. I won't be "almost done" for long.
